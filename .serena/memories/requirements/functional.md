# Functional requirements

- **FR-01.1 — Must Have:** Azure AI Document Intelligence extracts vendor, invoice date, invoice number, line-item descriptions, line-item amounts, total amount, and VAT amount. Display field-level extraction confidence. Flag any field below 85% for manual review. Phase-1 custom KU model training requires at least 20 petty-cash and 20 credit-card invoice samples.
- **FR-01.2 — Must Have:** Power Automate keeps petty-cash and credit-card processing separate and populates the matching tracking sheet. Each pipeline runs within five minutes after a new inbox document. SharePoint paths: `/InvoiceInbox/PettyCash/` and `/InvoiceInbox/CreditCard/`; trigger: SharePoint connector "When a file is created in a folder".
- **FR-01.3 — Must Have:** Admin review interface presents extraction next to invoice image/PDF for side-by-side validation. Staff can approve in one click or edit each field. Review completes within 24 hours after extraction; escalate after 24 hours. Implementation may use Power Apps or Power Automate approval workflow.
- **FR-01.4 — Must Have:** After each approved row, tracking sheets automatically calculate running totals, VAT totals, and month-to-date balance. No manual formulas. Use SharePoint List or Excel Online (cloud) for tracking data; Power Automate updates formulas/calculations.
- **FR-01.5 — Should Have:** Archive every processed invoice in structured SharePoint storage with vendor, date, amount, and invoice-type metadata. The archive must support instant search and comply with KU financial-records retention policy; it replaces unstructured storage and enables audit-ready retrieval.
